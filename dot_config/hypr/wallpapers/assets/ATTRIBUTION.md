# Silhouette assets

Traced silhouettes from [PhyloPic](https://www.phylopic.org/), rendered to alpha
masks. All are public domain; attribution below is courtesy, not a requirement.

| `owl.png` | *Phodilus badius* | John Gould & Henry C. Richter | Public Domain Mark 1.0 |
| `walker.png` | *Homo sapiens idaltu* | T. Michael Keesey | CC0 1.0 |
| `bird_soar.png` | *Falco tinnunculus* | Andy Wilson | CC0 1.0 |
| `bird_side.png` | *Corvus corax* | Andy Wilson | CC0 1.0 |
| `spruce_a.png` | *Picea abies* | Guillaume Dera | CC0 1.0 |
| `spruce_c.png` | *Picea abies* | Juri Felix | CC0 1.0 |

The tent and cabin are drawn from primitives in `generate.py`; everything else
in the wallpapers is generated procedurally.
